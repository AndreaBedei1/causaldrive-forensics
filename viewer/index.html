Causal structure

Time runs left to right; each band is one vehicle. An edge is a claim that the event on the left contributed to the one on the right. The ground truth is the observable one — the physical events a reconstruction could in principle recover — not the scenario script.

Responsibility findings

Causal and normative findings, not statements of legal fault. A contribution needs both a rule observed to be broken and an independent physical path from the vehicle's own behaviour to the outcome.

Temporal properties

Each formula is the object that was evaluated. A window that ran past the end of the recording, or across a gap in the evidence, is UNKNOWN rather than PASS.
