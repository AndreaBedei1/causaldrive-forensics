## Metric definitions

- `association_accuracy` (truth): correct / decided recorder-identity associations (n/a: none decided)
- `collision_recall` (truth): true vehicle-vehicle contacts reconstructed with exactly their participants
- `collision_detection` (truth): true contacts with a reconstructed collision node, participants right or not
- `wrong_collision_matches` (truth): detected contacts with wrong participants + reconstructed vehicle pairs without a true contact
- `unmatched_collision_reports` (truth): true contacts left without any reconstructed collision
- `runs_aligned` (truth): runs with every recorder on the global clock
- `max_relative_offset_error_s` (truth): largest error of a recorder's estimated clock offset
- `wrong_links` (truth): tracks linked to the wrong recorder
- `missed_links` (truth): tracks of a recorder left anonymous
- `false_merges` (truth): track pairs merged across different vehicles
- `missed_merges` (truth): track pairs of one vehicle left apart
- `phantom_entities` (truth): anonymous entities on no true vehicle
- `duplicate_hypotheses` (truth): extra entities of one true vehicle
- `centre_error_median_m` (truth): fused centre error of anonymous road users: per-run median, median over runs [m]
- `centre_error_p95_m` (truth): per-run p95, median over runs [m]
- `centre_error_max_m` (truth): largest fused centre error of any run [m]
- `velocity_error_median_mps` (truth): fused velocity error: per-run median, median over runs [m/s]
- `velocity_error_p95_mps` (truth): per-run p95, median over runs [m/s]
- `within_2sigma` (truth): share of fused states within their declared 2 sigma (Gaussian: 0.865)
- `ego_path_oscillations` (truth): EGO_PATH changes undone within 3 sweeps (audit)
- `track_losses` (truth): TRACK_LOST of confirmed tracks (audit)
- `fragmentation_losses` (truth): losses while the radar still saw the body
- `phantom_tracks` (truth): local tracks on no true vehicle (audit)
- `closure_violations` (truth): CRITICAL_TTC started after the pair's contact
- `cut_in_recall` (truth): CUT_IN against the audit's ground-truth criterion (n/a: no true cut-in)
- `oracle_event_recall` (truth): core events of the ground-truth coverage oracle found by the reconstruction
- `oracle_event_precision` (truth): reconstructed core events matched by the coverage oracle
- `event_preservation` (stability): core events of the unperturbed reconstruction found again
- `events_added` (stability): core events absent from the unperturbed reconstruction
- `identical_graphs` (stability): share of runs whose global graph is node for node the unperturbed one
- `event_dt_p95_s` (stability): per-run p95 time difference of the preserved events, median over runs [s]
- `absolute_pose_rms_m` (input): injected absolute GNSS error, RMS [m]
- `relative_pose_rms_m` (input): injected recorder-pair relative error, RMS [m]
- `association_accuracy 0-10 m` (truth): recorder links correct / (correct + wrong), tracks of median range in the band
- `association_accuracy 10-25 m` (truth): recorder links correct / (correct + wrong), tracks of median range in the band
- `association_accuracy >25 m` (truth): recorder links correct / (correct + wrong), tracks of median range in the band
- `incident_link_accuracy` (truth): tracks of incident participants that are recorders: correct link / (correct + wrong + missed)
- `incident_centre_error_median_m` (truth): fused centre error of non-recording incident participants
